PR6 capability — measured, then reinforced

This is proactive, non-clinical training on the Predictive 6 Factor Resilience (PR6) model by Hello Driven. It is for organisations that want people to hold under pressure without pretending that “be more resilient” is a strategy.

Delivery is by an HRD Corp Accredited Trainer and a Hello Driven Resilience Coach. HRD Corp claimable in Malaysia. Outcomes are measured; reinforcement continues in the app after the room empties.

The six domains

  • Vision — a usable sense of direction when the week is noisy.
  • Composure — regulating the body and the reaction before the meeting gets away from you.
  • Reasoning — thinking clearly enough to choose, not only to cope.
  • Tenacity — staying with the hard thing without burning the people around it.
  • Collaboration — asking, offering, and repairing so the load is not carried alone.
  • Health — the physical base that every other domain quietly depends on.

Who it is for

HR and L&D leads building a capability programme they can report on. Managers whose teams are still performing, but only just. Employers who know resilience training is not a substitute for fixing the work — and still need people who can hold while the system is being put right.

Preventative and non-clinical. This is workplace capability training, not therapy, psychiatric evaluation, or medical counselling. It does not replace statutory psychosocial-risk assessment. See PRisMA OSH Compliance for the system-level duty.
