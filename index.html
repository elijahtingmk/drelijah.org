Three Integrated Solutions

Clear Lines Between Leadership, Capability, and Compliance

Three distinct solutions, three organisational levers. The first is executive decision-making under uncertainty (Earned Leadership™). The second is proactive team resilience (PR6 model). The third is statutory OSH psychosocial risk screening (PRisMA, under DOSH PTP competency).

Pillar 01 · Leadership

Earned Leadership™
(赢心领导力)

A 14-hour executive decision engine for leaders facing uncertainty, decision constraints, and team alignment. Win hearts, not compliance.

  • The Fog Principle: Leading before outcomes are known
  • 3 Pillars: Decision, Communication, and Aura
  • 4-Level Earned Ladder: Positional to Earned Commitment
  • Delivered by an HRD Corp Accredited Trainer
Explore Earned Leadership →

Pillar 02 · Capability

Workplace Resilience Training

Proactive, non-clinical capability training based on the Predictive 6 Factor Resilience (PR6) model by Hello Driven.

  • Built on the evidence-based PR6 framework
  • Six domains: Vision, Composure, Reasoning, Tenacity, Collaboration, Health
  • Outcome measurement and app-based reinforcement
  • Delivered by an HRD Corp Accredited Trainer
Explore Resilience Training →

Pillar 03 · Compliance

PRisMA OSH Compliance Solutions

System-level psychosocial-risk screening for Malaysian employers carrying statutory obligations under OSHA 1994 (amended 2022), under DOSH PTP competency.

  • Stage I screening with LEO26 across work units
  • Stage II assessment with the EPC23 checklist
  • Risk classified at the system level
  • Defensible PRiMA Action Plan for DOSH
Explore PRisMA Compliance →

All three solutions are preventative and non-clinical. PRisMA screening is the statutory, OSH system-level element; none of the three constitutes clinical psychological therapy, psychiatric evaluation, or medical counselling.
