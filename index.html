Why this practice

Leadership is usually sold on performance. This one starts where the harm is — and where the law now sits.

Most programmes are justified by engagement, retention, and productivity. Useful, and incomplete. Psychosocial risk at work is now an employer duty in Malaysia. A great deal of that risk is decided by how people are led: who is handed the impossible choice, and what happens to the person who says it is impossible.

That is the research. That is also the audit trail. The work here is built so both can be true in the same room.
