How it works

Assessment first, then the decision in front of you

Dr. Elijah Ting coaches in Sibu and online. Each track starts with a validated assessment, then a structured conversation about what the results mean for the decision actually in front of you. Sponsoring employers receive a development summary agreed with the coachee — never the raw assessment data.

Three tracks

  • 01 Career Direct® — career direction For school leavers choosing a course, and adults at a mid-career crossroads. Personality, interests, skills, and values.
  • 02 WorkPlace Big Five® — leadership style For managers and executives. Trait and Leader reports worked through against delegation, conflict, and situational fit.
  • 03 WorkPlace Big Five® — competency For individual contributors and rising leaders. Which competencies are energising versus draining, and a plan around genuine strengths.

Developmental coaching, not therapy. It is not clinical psychological assessment, psychiatric evaluation, or medical care. Data is handled under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.
