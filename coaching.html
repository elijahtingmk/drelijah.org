Scope & Boundary

Developmental Coaching — Not Therapy

Coaching here is developmental and educational: it works on decisions, direction, and capability. It is not clinical psychological assessment, therapy, psychiatric evaluation, or medical care, and anyone who would be better served by clinical support is pointed towards a qualified provider. Assessment data is handled under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and is not shared with an employer without explicit consent.
